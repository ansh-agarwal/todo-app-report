Todo App Project · Strategy Brief

A Better Cross-Platform Todo App

Full research synthesis — 16-competitor landscape, market pulse, demographics, engineering deep-dives, and the working product thesis. Compiled from 7 research passes (2026-08-06 / 07). Built for fast review.

The Working Thesis

Build against a three-legged gap that no incumbent occupies all of at once. Each of the 16 apps studied gives up at least one leg.

1 · Capture = Retrieval

Notes and todos are the same underlying object. Zero-config: a captured item becomes findable later via passive semantic surfacing — not a chat box you must remember to query. Solves the universal "capture graveyard" failure.

2 · AI = Connective Tissue

AI links, tags, and resurfaces; turns voice/meeting capture into a note and a task in one motion. It explicitly does not auto-manage your calendar or play "executive assistant" — over-automation is actively resented (see Motion). AI does the boring structuring, never decides what matters.

3 · Flat, Transparent Pricing

$8–12/mo single tier, AI included, no credit metering. Undercuts Sunsama/Amie/Akiflow/Motion; beats free-tier TickTick/Twos on trust. Metered AI credits (Notion, ClickUp) are actively backfiring in 2026.

The bet, in one line

A single-price, capture-first tool where notes and todos are one object, AI passively links/tags/resurfaces without being asked, voice/meeting capture converts into both a note and a task, and the product refuses to auto-run your calendar. That combination isn't fully occupied by any of the 16 apps studied.

Three design decisions already locked (Anshul, 2026-08-07)

💰 DESI-5 · Pricing/Architecture

Let the user bring the AI. On-device model or their own API key → our marginal inference cost ≈ 0 → app stays free & trustworthy.

  • On-device AI runs always-on stuff (semantic recall, auto-linking/tagging via embeddings) — the "passive connective tissue," and a privacy moat cloud-locked incumbents can't match.
  • BYOK for heavy generative work (summaries, chat) — user pays the provider directly.
  • Free = manual app + on-device passive AI + BYOK. Paid (~$8-12/mo flat) = we run the cloud AI so non-technical users need no key. Selling convenience, not access.

🧭 DESI-6 · Phase-1 Beachhead

English-speaking ADHD / overwhelm niche (US/EU + global English) via Reddit, Product Hunt, X. India = phase 2.

  • ADHD app market $1.15B→$3.66B (~18% CAGR, faster than ~10% general).
  • The one segment incumbents demonstrably fail — users fleeing Notion/Todoist to Saner.ai/Noplex.
  • WTP proven: professional/high-intent segments convert 5–10% vs 2.2% freemium avg.
  • The ADHD spec (calm, low-load, capture-first, offline-tolerant) = the same spec India needs later.

🎯 DESI-4 · Product Thesis

The three legs above, built AGAINST the gap. Companion research: market-pulse.md market-intel.md demographics.md.

Secondary wedge: "overwhelmed brain / ADHD-first" positioning is underserved — only Saner.ai + Twos, both small.

Build principles (from the engineering deep-dives)

  • Local-first is the bigger lever than native rendering for "snappy." Things 3's instant feel comes from local edits + background sync — never blocking the UI on the network.
  • Derived-cache-from-source-of-truth with incremental invalidation is Obsidian's real speed trick — not a fancy DB. Our objects are atomic (one capture/task), so re-embedding on edit is cheap by construction.
  • Native mobile is the clearest unsolved gap — Notion's mobile is a "read-only viewer"; wrapped-WebView (Obsidian/Capacitor) fails on cold-start + sync. Budget for genuinely native mobile.
  • Shallow, opinionated hierarchy + soft defaults ("This Evening", "Someday") reduce decision fatigue — calm by design, directly serving the ADHD beachhead.

Where AI genuinely helps (survives "turn it off")
  • Passive, zero-prompt semantic recall (Obsidian Smart Connections) — the one AI pattern reviewers keep.
  • Voice-to-task / voice-to-note (Superlist, Reflect Whisper, Saner.ai) — turns talking into structured data.
  • Auto-scheduling that actually re-slots (Motion) — irreplaceable when it works; removes a decision.
  • Light auto-enrichment (Twos: address→map, movie→showtimes) — invisible until needed.
  • Meeting summarization — the least controversial "saves real hours" feature across the whole category.
Where AI is lipstick (turned off)
  • Generic in-app chat bolted onto a workspace (Notion AI = "wrapper around GPT/Claude that only sees your data").
  • "AI Employee" overpromise (Akiflow's Aki) — marketing-vs-reality gap is the top disappointment.
  • Auto-reschedule-your-life as default — Sunsama's refusal to do it is praised as a feature.
  • Per-seat/per-credit AI billing on already-paid tools — a trust problem that kills adoption.

Pattern: AI wins when it removes a step the user already did manually (transcribe, tag, link, enrich). It loses when presented as replacing a judgment call (reschedule my day, decide what matters) — or when its billing creates more anxiety than the time it saves.

The seven signals that matter

Category is bifurcating, not consolidating

Splitting into "AI-native personal RAG" vs. "old-school lightweight" — no winner-take-all shape.

"AI feature fatigue" is the dominant narrative

Users ask "is this actually helping?" not "which AI next?" 2026 rule of thumb: use AI for boring work, not decisions.

"Consolidation fatigue" is the loudest complaint

Tired of switching across 5-7 tools per project. The market wants consolidation that reduces cognitive load — not ClickUp, which consolidates features but not load.

Capture-vs-retrieval is the structural failure

"Every app is built for capturing, almost none for finding." The "capture graveyard" recurs across Evernote, Notion, Obsidian post-mortems.

Subscription fatigue is quantified, not grumbling

5-year cost deltas openly compared ($447 one-time vs $6,490 over 5 years). Metered/credit AI add-ons resisted — exactly where Notion/ClickUp/Motion moved.

Billing trust > price

Akiflow & Motion's loudest complaints are surprise charges, not sticker price. Pushing users toward simplest flat pricing (TickTick, Reflect, Twos).

ADHD/overwhelm is a fast-growing wedge

Saner.ai & Twos both cite it as their sharpest differentiator + highest-conviction base. "Designed for an overwhelmed brain" is a viable GTM.

Reddit/PH threads recommend stacks, not single tools

"Beyond Time + Reclaim + Grammarly" — implicitly conceding no single app nailed capture+task+AI at once. That's a gap, not a settled market.

Who It's For — Demographics & Beachhead

Recommended beachhead: ADHD/overwhelm-friendly productivity, English high-WTP first, India phase 2. Fast growth + explicit incumbent neglect = the classic beachhead signal.

The five personas

A · Overwhelmed Knowledge Worker largest · saturated

60% of time goes to "work about work"; 275 interruptions/day. Highest WTP (5-10% convert, often employer-expensed). But every incumbent targets it — hardest beachhead despite biggest pool.

D · ADHD / Neurodivergent ★ recommended

$1.15B→$3.66B market; ~37M adult users. Standard todo UX actively fails them. WTP demonstrated (they pay for therapeutic-adjacent value). ~29% face connectivity limits → offline-first is a requirement. The most explicitly "underserved" segment across all research.

C · Solo Founder / Solopreneur growing

23.7%→36.3% of startups (2019→2025). 54% burnout, 75% anxiety — the #1 failure predictor. Will pay for anything that credibly replaces headcount. Underserved wedge: calm/low-friction, not another dashboard.

B · Student low WTP

Classic "free forever" cohort; monetizes later as they graduate. Strategic funnel value, not direct revenue. Avoid as v1.

E · Team Lead / Manager

Requires multiplayer from day one — heavier build. Lower priority as a beachhead.

India specifics (phase 2)

  • 783M smartphone users, overwhelmingly mobile-first — Android/Play Store is the default channel. Excellent Android before anything else.
  • Brutal price sensitivity: ceiling ~₹99–199/mo (~$1.20–2.40); Netflix India-optimal ~₹300. Won't sustain a paid tier at launch → BYOK/local-first free model (DESI-5) fits perfectly.

  • Phones default to English; regional-language is a longer-term bet, not v1.

The elegance: the ADHD spec (calm, low-load, capture-first, offline-tolerant) is the India spec (mobile-first, connectivity-constrained, price-sensitive). Build once → monetize West in phase 1 → expand India in phase 2. Overwhelm/burnout is the shared hook that generalizes the wedge into the $13B knowledge-worker + solo-founder markets — a wedge, not a dead-end.

Engineering & UX Deep-Dives

Four studies pressure-testing the thesis: how the best apps actually stay fast, and where each one leaves a gap.

🟣 Obsidian — the speed trick & the local-AI proof

Files are the database. Each note = a plain .md file; metadata in YAML frontmatter. No SQLite, no relational DB.

  • The speed secret = the Metadata Cache: parse each file once into a structured record; query the cache, never re-parse raw markdown. Parse-once/query-many + incremental invalidation + event-driven (not polling). Persisted in IndexedDB, re-derived from disk if lost.
  • Deliberately not block-based (unlike Roam/Logseq) — the unit is the file, keeping the invalidation graph small. ~100× faster load than block-based rivals.
  • Smart Connections = the closest existing proof of DESI-5: zero-config local embeddings (transformers.js, ~25MB BGE model, no API key, fully offline). JSON + brute-force cosine similarity — nobody reaches for a vector DB at vault scale.
  • Its weakness we beat: it re-embeds the whole file on any edit. Our objects are atomic (one capture/task) → re-embedding is cheap by construction. Positioning: "instant recall because your notes are already atomic."
  • Where the paywall sits: Smart Connections gates passive surfacing-while-you-write at $60/yr, not raw search. Tells us exactly where premium value lives: ranking/relevance/timing, not access.
  • Mobile is its clearest unsolved problem — same web codebase wrapped in Capacitor/WebView → 60s+ cold starts on large vaults, sync fragility, ~30% of plugins don't work on mobile. If native snappiness matters, wrapped-WebView is a known-risk path.
🔵 Things 3 & Sublime Text — snappiness & calm UX

Things 3: its "instant" feel is local-first + background sync, not exotic rendering — the app never blocks the UI on a network round-trip.

  • Calm by removing choices: shallow opinionated hierarchy (vs OmniFocus's infinite nesting), NL date parsing, soft buckets ("This Evening", "Someday"). The "Someday" list is a psychological feature — defer without guilt.
  • Avoid: Apple-only lock-in and opaque sync — conflicts with our cross-platform bet.

Sublime Text: purpose-built primitives on the hottest path — custom UI toolkit (Skyline), custom regex engine (sregex, tests many regexes at once), LevelDB symbol index, GPU rendering.

  • Steal: "do the work once, ahead of time, reuse it" — precompute/persist a bespoke local index for our capture/retrieval hot path.
  • Avoid: over-building custom toolkits early — Sublime could afford years of it via a patient, profitable, non-VC model. Default to native toolkits; go bespoke only where profiling proves it's the differentiator.
  • "No abstraction tax": every framework layer in the critical path (web view, generic ORM, network round-trip) leaks perceived latency.
⚪ Apple (Reminders+Notes) vs Google (Keep+Tasks) — the capture bar
  • Apple = fastest capture: Action Button / Siri / Lock-Screen Quick Note → item created in <2s, no app switch; widget-level completion; true offline-first. But: zero cross-platform, and Reminders ≠ Notes (two separate objects — exactly the fragmentation we bet against).
  • Google = real cross-platform (Android/iOS/web) but degraded on iOS (no widget). Keep = capture, Tasks = structured — split across two apps.
  • Google validates DESI-4 directly: mid-migration (2025→2026) folding Keep reminders into Tasks because the split caused user confusion — at the cost of losing location-based reminders. Even Google is paying to undo the note/task split.
  • The open lane: nobody has "native-feeling snappiness on both iOS and Android." And none offer local-first/BYOK — all locked to iCloud or Google account.

Capture features to match: system-level single-gesture entry, widget capture+completion, voice create and query, share-sheet ingest, silent offline-first sync.

🟦 Microsoft (To Do + OneNote) — a $2T company's broken bridge
  • To Do: good "My Day" ritual + Outlook capture, but no project structure, chronically slow web app (years unfixed → org-priority issue), and no AI investment (that goes to OneNote/Copilot).
  • OneNote (2026): betting big on AI inside the note surface — Copilot Notebooks, multimodal capture → structured "Copilot Page." Validates "capture → structured actionable output" as the market direction.
  • The live gap: the OneNote↔To Do task-sync bridge is currently broken post-New-Outlook, with unresolved complaints. If a $2T company can't wire note→task continuity across its own two apps, that's strong signal the unified model is both valuable and hard enough to be a moat.
📣 Community sentiment — "I wish an app would just…"
  • "…capture in under 5 seconds without deciding where it goes." The single most-repeated requirement. "If saving a note takes more than five seconds, you won't do it consistently."
  • "…turn my note into a task without re-typing it." The current frontier; nobody's delivered it well.
  • "…not make me manage a system on top of managing my life." ADHD-specific: more choice is the problem, not the cure → our AI must reduce visible complexity, not add knobs.
  • "…work as well on mobile as desktop." Recurs for Notion/Evernote/OneNote — architectural debt they can't easily retrofit.
  • "…not lock AI behind a sub when I already pay for the AI myself." BYOK moving niche→expected.
  • "…stop breaking sync between its own apps." Trust in sync is fragile even among giants.

The Gaps We Can Win On

Four converging signals point at the same opening. Each is validated by a competitor's specific, current failure — not speculation.

1 · Note→task continuity, claimed cleanly by nobody

Microsoft has two apps + a currently-broken bridge. Google is mid-migration undoing its own Keep/Tasks split. Notion pivoting to team AI-agent orchestration, away from personal capture. Evernote/OneNote bolted AI onto notes but never fused note+task into one object. DESI-4 is the differentiator, not a "me too."

2 · Mobile-native speed, unaddressed at scale

Notion's mobile = explicit "read-only viewer" in 2026; OneNote lags on large files; web-wrapper architectures make this hard to fix without a rewrite. A truly native, fast mobile capture experience is a wedge that isn't closing on its own — it's architectural debt competitors are stuck with.

3 · BYOK-free / managed-paid, validated but unowned

Evernote = managed-only (no BYOK). Obsidian proves BYOK works & is loved — but plugin-fragmented, not native. Nobody offers "BYOK unlocks it free, or pay us to manage it" as one clean, native, first-party toggle. Real gap for privacy/cost-conscious users cobbling together Obsidian + a paid plugin today.

4 · Pricing trust as a feature

Motion & Akiflow bleeding users over billing opacity even where the product works. A single flat price with AI included (not metered) is a genuine differentiator against the entire Notion/ClickUp/Motion credit-billing direction. Doing the less-sophisticated pricing thing is the more credible move.

Runner-up wedge worth tracking

The ADHD/overwhelm angle (DESI-6): most competitors are adding configurability and surface area, which ADHD sentiment explicitly calls the wrong direction. A product that gets simpler as it gets smarter — rather than adding settings — is differentiated against the entire market's current trajectory.

The synthesized bet

Single flat price (no credit metering) · capture-first where note = todo (one object) · AI passively links/tags/resurfaces without being asked · voice/meeting capture → both a note and a task in one motion · explicitly refuses to auto-manage your calendar. Built native-first for mobile snappiness, local-first + BYOK free / managed paid. Beachhead: English ADHD/overwhelm → generalize to knowledge-workers & solo-founders → India phase 2.
